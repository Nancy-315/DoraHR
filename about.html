About DoraHR

DoraHR is an MBA-focused HR assistant designed to help students understand HR concepts, prepare academic projects, create questionnaires, work on internship reports, prepare for viva examinations and learn practical HR concepts.

Key Academic Support Areas

📊 MBA Projects & Dissertations

Guidance on research topics, SMART objectives, sampling methods, Likert questionnaires, statistical tools, and project viva preparation.

💼 Internship Reports

Structuring executive summaries, industry profiles, HR workflow descriptions, weekly diary reflections, and learning outcomes.

⚖️ Labour Laws & Compliance

Clear explanations of the Factories Act, Industrial Disputes Act, PF, ESI, Gratuity, POSH Act 2013, and Indian Labour Codes.

📈 HR Analytics & Systems

Practical insights into HR metrics, employee turnover rate, eNPS, recruitment analytics, and SAP HCM / HRIS fundamentals.

Academic Notice: DoraHR is an educational learning assistant designed for study and research assistance. DoraHR is not an official product of any university or company.
